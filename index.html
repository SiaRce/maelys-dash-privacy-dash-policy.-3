Maëlys AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Maëlys AI is a personal AI assistant designed to help users manage emails and daily activities. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, and protected.

2. Yahoo Mail Access

With the user's explicit authorization, Maëlys AI may connect to Yahoo Mail using approved authentication methods. This access may allow the application to display emails, assist with summaries, prepare replies, and send messages when authorized.

3. How Information Is Used

Email information is used only to provide requested features, including displaying messages, summarizing content, and helping users compose responses.

4. AI Processing

When users request AI assistance, relevant information may be processed by an AI service to generate summaries or responses. Email content is not intentionally transmitted to AI services without the user's requested action.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Maëlys AI aims to minimize data storage. Information is retained only as necessary to provide functionality or comply with applicable legal requirements.

6. Data Sharing

Maëlys AI does not sell personal information or email content. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application.

7. Security

The application aims to protect information through secure authentication, encrypted connections, and appropriate access restrictions. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. User Rights

Users may disconnect their email account, revoke authorization, and request deletion of stored information.

9. Children's Privacy

Maëlys AI is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Maëlys AI develops. Updated versions will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer of Maëlys AI through the contact information provided with the application.
